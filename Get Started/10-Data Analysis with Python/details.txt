## Rolling_mean:

rolling_mean = df.rolling(window=7).mean()
df.rolling(window=7) → Creates a moving window of 7 rows (in your case, 7 days, since your index is daily).

.mean() → For each position of the window, Pandas computes the average of the values inside that window.

Step-by-step example
Suppose your data column is:

yaml
Copy
Edit
Date       data
2021-01-01   10
2021-01-02   20
2021-01-03   30
2021-01-04   40
2021-01-05   50
2021-01-06   60
2021-01-07   70
2021-01-08   80
Now apply rolling(window=3).mean() (3-day rolling mean):

First 2 rows → NaN (not enough values yet for a full window)

Row 3 (Jan 3): mean(10,20,30) = 20

Row 4 (Jan 4): mean(20,30,40) = 30

Row 5 (Jan 5): mean(30,40,50) = 40

Row 6 (Jan 6): mean(40,50,60) = 50

Row 7 (Jan 7): mean(50,60,70) = 60

Row 8 (Jan 8): mean(60,70,80) = 70

Result:
